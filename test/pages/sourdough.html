Keeping a sourdough starter alive

A sourdough starter is a mix of flour and water that wild yeast and bacteria turn into a leavening agent. Feed it equal weights of flour and water once a day if it lives on the counter, or once a week if it lives in the fridge.

A healthy starter doubles in size four to eight hours after a feed and smells pleasantly sour. A layer of grey liquid on top, called hooch, means it is hungry: pour it off and feed it.

Whole wheat or rye flour makes a starter more active, and chlorinated tap water can slow it down. If it smells like nail polish remover, feed it more often.
